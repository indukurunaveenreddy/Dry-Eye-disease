Core Modules

🌟 Key Capabilities

Six integrated AI-powered clinical modules for comprehensive dry eye assessment.

👁️
Infrared Meibography Screening
GLCM spatial texture extraction, gland dropout detection, and ML classification across 999 non-contact scans.
GLCM + ML Classifier
📹
Live Camera Ocular Tracking
Real-time Haar cascade facial landmark detection with automated ocular cropping and gaze classification.
OpenCV + Haar Cascade
📋
12-Item OSDI Neural Modeling
MLP classification predicting symptom severity: Normal, Mild, Moderate, Severe from 230-patient OSDI-12 cohort.
MLP Neural Network
⚡
Blink Completeness & Gaze Dynamics
VGG-19 transfer learning classifying 6 ocular orientations across 10,760 video frames.
VGG-19 Transfer Learning
🤖
OculoAI Clinical Chatbot
Interactive consultation assistant with dry eye guidance and 20-20-20 visual hygiene recommendations.
NLP Chatbot Engine

Multimodal Datasets Hub
